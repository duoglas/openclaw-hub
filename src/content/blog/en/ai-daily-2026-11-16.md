## (November 14 backfill) South Korea says Nvidia reaffirmed its 260,000-GPU plan

In its November 14, 2026 report, TechCrunch cited South Korea’s Science Ministry as saying Nvidia had reaffirmed plans to supply 260,000 advanced GPUs to the country and continue joint AI projects. The report placed that statement in the context of Jensen Huang’s departure. This establishes a ministry-reported continuation of the plan, not a new order or a count of GPUs already delivered.

The statement directly concerns South Korea’s joint AI projects and chip-supply relationship with Nvidia. TechCrunch also noted that Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix are important suppliers of memory chips used in Nvidia’s AI processors. The relationship therefore spans both GPU supply and upstream memory components, rather than a single procurement transaction.

Citing local media, TechCrunch also reported that South Korea’s government AI strategy council held an emergency meeting to assess the potential impact of the leadership change on the domestic AI industry. The reaffirmed plan sits alongside an ongoing government assessment. It provides a limited indication of continuity, but the supplied text gives no GPU models, delivery schedule or project allocations from which to establish when Korean teams would gain access to additional compute.
